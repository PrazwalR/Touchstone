### VolatilePair reference

Use the published worked-example fee configuration, fixed across windows. Its spread is 900 pips split across bid and ask at initialization. Label this comparator as our implementation of the spec. The versioned specification has SHA-256 `36b0ec77b03b8690a908403f78f5f0338df5904f657bb62f6f4e898a0bd88708`. The model uses the pre-swap sqrt price, zero-for-one direction, original block ordering, Q48 rounding, block-open skew, temporary fee and mid-block displacement. [Pinned upstream specification](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-hooks-public/blob/82de6aa6de5c3e387fa5a088af6977f14f66e314/docs/technical/VolatilePairHook.md)
