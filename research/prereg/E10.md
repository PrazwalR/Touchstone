# E10 pre-registration draft · Arbitrage licence

Status: **draft**. No E10 tape or verdict.

Compare the plain clawback, one bidder paying the reserve, and two or more bidders on identical outside-price observations. Include both active-holder and idle-holder runs. Label the race and its economics as simulation. Real bidder and holder transactions belong in a separate testnet demonstration.

## Model

Use a constant-product pool and a quote-notional size grid at or above N₀. A model block admits one eligible first touch. Each arbitrageur arrives after its sampled latency, computes net profit using only the current observation and can abstain. The first profitable arrival wins. Resolve the deposit with the later settlement price and LP net with the separate 15-second price. Disclose this first-touch model, the fresh reference assumption and the absence of concentrated ticks, intra-block backruns and retail flow. The source for the race and auction primitives is `simulations/licence.py`.

For each scenario, reuse price paths, latency draws and costs across policies. Value each bidder by the profit difference between acting as holder and acting as outsider. Report reserve nonparticipation. One qualifying bidder pays the reserve, and competing qualifying bidders pay the second valuation. Pay rent only for the modeled lease term and subtract the configured protocol and rebate allocations before crediting LPs. Report LP net, rent per day, holder win rate among filled opportunities, and idle-holder mean absolute gap in basis points.

The licence stays off unless the competitive case beats plain LP income by at least 10% and the one-bidder reserve case does no worse. Kill if the idle-holder mean deviation exceeds G plus the fee band. Report undefined comparisons separately, including empty fills. A nonpositive plain income makes a relative gain ambiguous and needs an explicit convention before freeze.

## Draft path implementation, 6 November 2026

`simulations/licence_path.py` now carries reserves through successive first-touch races. The caller supplies
each block's observation, settlement and 15-second markout prices, ordered agent arrivals, the end time,
trade-size grid, terms, holder and rent rate. Equal arrival times keep the supplied agent order; the eventual
scenario must register that tie policy. Future prices affect realized cash flows but cannot choose a trade.

The path reports LP trading income and rent separately, bidder profits after rent, holder wins among fills,
and time-weighted post-race absolute deviation in basis points. Each post-race price is held until the next
observation. This discrete approximation ignores the within-block waiting time before the winning arrival.
An idle holder is removed from the race but pays the specified rent for the full modeled term. This assumes
a funded lease without lapse, displacement, or deposit-capital costs. Fees, donations and rent do not compound
the constant-product reserves. Benign rebates and settlement-budget timing remain unmodeled.

`bidder_value` compares two paths from identical initial reserves and identical observations/arrival draws,
before rent. The outsider counterfactual must be supplied explicitly: another holder, or plain clawback.
That choice must be fixed in the registration; they are different economic comparisons. Auction clearing
still uses `clearing_rent`, whose valuation unit is total quote profit over the entire lease. Convert the
cleared total into a per-second rent using the same lease duration before a paid replay.

Only synthetic unit tests have run. There is no dataset loader, frozen sensitivity grid or E10 verdict.

## Inputs still required before freeze

Register the price windows and warm-up, block cadence, liquidity, trade sizes, agent counts, latency and hedging-cost distributions, gas, reserve rents, seed, and handling of benign-record rebates and idle-holder rent. Each bidder needs an explicit outsider counterfactual on the same path. The simulator's primitives and synthetic tests alone do not price the licence or establish a deployment verdict.
