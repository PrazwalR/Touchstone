# E11 pre-registration draft · Rebate coverage and farming

Status: **draft**. No pool concentration measurement or E11 verdict.

## Historical backtest

Use the same development and held-out windows as E3. Replay settlements in order with separate balances for each pool and currency. Start with an empty rebate budget. Apply the configured funding fraction ρ to the eligible retained proceeds and cap each eligible rebate by the available balance and κ times its fee. Report eligible fee volume, requested rebates, paid rebates, unpaid demand and ending balance by currency. Report gross coverage as funded demand (trader receipts plus keeper tips) over requested rebates, undefined when there was no demand. Also report net trader receipts and tips separately. The budget and active-owner share calculations are in `simulations/rebates.py`. The chosen default parameters in §14 are targets of 10% for ρ and 40% for κ.

## Simulation

Replay pure wash round trips, LP self-wash at measured active liquidity concentration, and a split arbitrage with the same starting inventory, ending inventory and outside-price path as its unsplit control. Account for both legs, LP fee recovery, impact, gas, retained value and rebates actually fundable at the time. Separate ordinary arbitrage profit from incremental farming profit against that control. For LP self-wash aggregate positions belonging to the same identifiable owner at the active tick and block. Report custodial positions with unresolved beneficial ownership separately.

The kill line from §16.4 is pool-specific. Kill rebates on any pool where a wash strategy profits at its configured κ. Report each strategy in each window. A pool without measured concentration or settlement history cannot receive a passing verdict.

Before freezing, pin the liquidity ownership source, sampling, cost and split grids, and counterfactual execution code. Commit the analysis before freezing the preregistration and accessing experiment inputs.
