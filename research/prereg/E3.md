## Disclosure before freezing

The held-out window was already used by E2 (with Binance prices), so this run is not blind to the window. While
building and checking the code, before any Pyth data for these windows was downloaded, the following were run
and seen, using Binance-derived stand-ins for the Pyth prices:

1. A dry run with the spec defaults, before the grid existed: held-out cap share about 0.22, retail fee about
   5.04 bp. This is why the grid and selection rule were added; spec §14.1 assigns delta, delta_max and beta to E3.
2. A dry run of the grid on the development window and two held-out points. Cap shares on the held-out window
   differed sharply from the development window, which led to the discovery of the ITM-1 bug (the in-the-money
   leg was larger than the swap on 1,902 of 43,080 held-out records). That fix was later replaced by the repair's actual-receipts rule (decision 0045); bounding the overshot leg is open (decision 0049).
3. After the fix, cap shares with Binance prices for three points: development (30, 300, 1.25) 0.35,
   (50, 300, 3) 0.30, (150, 300, 3) 0.27, driven by one $4.1M swap on 21 Sep 2026 at a 764 bp gap; held-out
   0.081, 0.026 and 0.000.

The grid and the selection rule were fixed before item 3 and were not changed after it.

## Scope reconciliation before renewed freeze (decision 0052)

Owner decision on 1 November 2026: preserve current formulas and disclose approximate replay. The six differences are reconciled in decision 0052: actual receipts for non-overshoots and two-step risky deposit rounding now match the contract; overshooting constant-liquidity reconstruction remains approximate across ticks. A single quote-valued rebate buffer, exact-input assumption, no outstanding-cap occupancy and swap-order settlement remain deliberate modelling differences. Previous-swap liquidity and inherited E1/E2 reference timing limitations also remain. E3 is a fixed-flow counterfactual, not an exact stateful v4 replay.

The disclosure above is retained verbatim. The experiment's thresholds, grid and selection rule have not changed. No historical datasets or legacy Pyth cache exist on this checkout; reported older diagnostics describe prior work and were not rerun. Renewing this freeze does not make an already seen window blind.
