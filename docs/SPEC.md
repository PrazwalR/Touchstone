The specifications are design references. Some examples, milestone descriptions and deployment details differ from the current implementation. Consult the contract source, decision records and deployment manifests for executable behavior and deployed addresses.

Implemented decisions include isolated payout delivery, authenticated executed-fee snapshots, non-overshooting in-the-money accounting and factory-owned pool configuration. Experimental thresholds and recorded E1/E2 results remain unchanged.

Research results, simulated behavior and local test measurements are distinct evidence. See the [research results](../research/results/README.md) and [threat model](threat-model.md) before interpreting a feature as economically validated.
